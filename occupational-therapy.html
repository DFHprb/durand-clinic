How I Can Help

Understanding What Makes Writing Hard

As an Occupational Therapist, Bela provides individualized therapy for children ages pre-K to Grade 5 who are experiencing challenges with handwriting (print and cursive), written output, fine-motor and visual-motor skills, attention, organization, and functional participation.

Her approach goes beyond handwriting mechanics to understand what is making a task difficult for the child and to develop practical, individualized strategies that build skills, confidence, and independence.

  • ✓ Handwriting assessment and intervention
  • ✓ Fine-motor and visual-motor skill development
  • ✓ Written-output support
  • ✓ Strategies to improve classroom and everyday participation

A Holistic OT Perspective

Beyond the Handwriting

The goal is not simply to improve handwriting. The goal is to understand what is making the task difficult and provide the right supports so your child can participate more successfully.

This may involve considering handwriting mechanics, fine-motor and visual-motor skills, attention, task initiation, endurance, regulation, organization, self-confidence and environmental supports. Recommendations are individualized to your child’s strengths, needs and daily environments.
